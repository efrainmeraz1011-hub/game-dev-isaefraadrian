# Richelieu class battleships (France)

Type: fast battleship. Nation: France, Marine nationale; Richelieu served under Vichy control at Dakar and then under Free French and Allied command from December 1942 [S35]. The class was France's answer to the Italian Littorio class, ordered after Italy announced two 35,000-ton ships with 381 mm guns on 11 June 1935 [S10, pp. 94-95].

## Design lineage

The design was a scaled-up Dunkerque, chosen on 27 November 1935 from six proposals because it best balanced speed, armament and protection inside the 35,000-ton limit [S10, pp. 95-97]. Both main battery turrets sit forward, each split into two halves by an armoured bulkhead [S10, pp. 99, 101-102, 106]. The compact "Sural" pressure-fired boilers shortened the machinery spaces and the armoured citadel by almost 5 m [S10, pp. 97-98]. Clemenceau kept the layout with a rearranged secondary battery; Gascogne moved one main turret aft [S10, pp. 166-167].
