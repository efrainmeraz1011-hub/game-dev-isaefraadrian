## What differed from the class baseline

- Lead ship and flagship of Battleship Division 7 on deployment to the Pacific in January 1944 [S281]; flew the flag of Vice Admiral Willis A. Lee, Commander Battleships Pacific, at the Mili bombardment of 18 March 1944 [S281].
- Carried President Roosevelt to and from Casablanca in November 1943 [S281] [S287], during which the destroyer William D. Porter fired a live torpedo at her by accident on 14 November 1943 [S321].
- The only Iowa-class ship hit by shore fire in the war: two Japanese 4.7-inch projectiles at Mili on 18 March 1944, damage negligible [S281].
- Halsey's flagship for the surrender ceremony of 2 September 1945 and 5th Fleet flagship from January 1946 [S281].
- Overhauled at San Francisco from 15 January 1945 [S281]; the DANFS entry does not say what the overhaul changed.
