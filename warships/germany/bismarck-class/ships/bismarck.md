# Bismarck

Status: partial (see gaps.md)

Bismarck is the class baseline for this dossier. Every facet file describes her unless it names Tirpitz. This note holds her build identity, the fittings that were hers alone, and the ship-specific documents found.

## What was hers and not Tirpitz's

- Machinery. Three Blohm & Voss turbine sets, no cruising turbine fitted; maximum obtained 150,170 hp and 30.12 knots [S5; S1]. Tirpitz had Brown Boveri sets with a cruising turbine [S5, footnote 2; S38].
- Heavy anti-aircraft mounts. A mixed fit: four forward Dop. L. C/31d and four after Dop. L. C/37, the after C/37 mounts installed at Gotenhafen in October or November 1940 [S11; S20]. navypedia states that Tirpitz had all sixteen 10.5 cm guns in C/37 mounts while Bismarck and every earlier German capital ship had C/31 [S38, project history]. NavWeaps suggests the mixture, whose training and elevating characteristics the fire control apparently did not allow for, may partly explain her poor anti-aircraft showing [S11, mount note 7b], and the AVKS asked for early uniformity [S16, ¶90].
- Turret Anton. Alone of the four it had a rotary cartridge-case ejector drum in place of the flap cover [S9, mount data]. Its 10.5 m rangefinder was removed at Hamburg in the winter of 1940/41 because sea water fogged it at speed [S6, footnote 1], and the AVKS reported in May 1941 that the removal "still had not been done" and could be abandoned [S16, ¶105].
- No torpedo tubes and no mines were carried [S15, §VII(1)].
- Light anti-aircraft. Two 2 cm C/38 quadruple mounts were fitted beside the foremast, above the searchlight platform, in late April 1941 [S4, footnote 1; S20]. navypedia records for March 1941 one added single 2 cm C/38 and presumably a fourth FuMO 23, and for May 1941 the two quadruples plus five single 2 cm C/38 [S38, modernizations]; the count of 2 cm guns is disputed and the conflicts are listed in `03-armament-and-ammunition.md`.
- Radar. Three FuMO 23, one on each command post [S1; S6]. NavWeaps notes that a fourth mattress antenna on the front of her conning tower, always covered by canvas in photographs, is disputed in the German literature [S14, Bismarck class].
- Propellers and degaussing. Her three propellers and the MES magnetic self-protection system were installed in floating dry dock V-VI between 23 June and 14 July 1940 [S20; S5].
- Inclining test of 21 July 1940: GM 3.96 m in the "empty ship as completed" condition at 42,663 tons [S20].

## Complement

103 officers including ensigns and 1,962 men as commissioned, 2,065 in all [S29]. For Rheinübung more than 2,200 were aboard with the Fleet Commander's staff, prize crews and war correspondents [S29]; prisoners put the number at 2,300 to 2,400 [S15, §II]. navypedia gives 2,092 [S38]. Losses and rescue counts are in `06-crew-and-daily-life.md`.
