# Sources, facet 03 (armament and ammunition handling)

Numbered S81 to S103. Tiers: 1 primary, 2 reference, 3 museum or enthusiast site. Every entry was fetched on 2026-09-11.

S81. 16-Inch Three Gun Turrets BB-61 Class, Ordnance Pamphlet 769 (NAVORD OP 769). Bureau of Ordnance, US Navy. Scanned at Gene Slover's US Navy Pages, edition dated 15 October 1967 for the 30 April 1968 reactivation of USS New Jersey (BB-62); it supersedes OP 769 (Preliminary) of June 1943 and the First Revision of December 1953. Index page https://eugeneleeslover.com/ENGINEERING/OP769.html; scans https://www.eugeneleeslover.com/AMMUNITION/NAVORD-OP-769_part1.pdf, _part2.pdf, _part3.pdf. Tier 1. Accessed 2026-09-11. Pages used: 1-1 to 1-4, 1-7, 1-9, 1-19, 1-27, 2-1 to 2-12, 2-21 to 2-24, 2-26, 2-32 to 2-34, 3-1 to 3-3, 4-1, 7-1, 7-2, 8-1, 9-1, 9-3, 10-1, 10-2, 11-1 to 11-3, A1-1, A2-1, A2-2, A3-1, A4-1 to A4-3; figure 2-1 (firing cycle dial). All numbers from this source describe the 1968 New Jersey configuration unless the text says otherwise.
