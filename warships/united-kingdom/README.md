# Royal Navy battleships and battlecruisers, 1939 to 1945

In September 1939 the Royal Navy had twelve battleships and three battlecruisers in commission: two Nelson class ships completed in 1927 [R2], five Queen Elizabeth class completed 1915 to 1916 [R3], five Revenge class completed 1916 to 1917 [R4], the two Renown class battlecruisers completed in 1916 [R13, R14], and Hood, completed 15 May 1920 [R20]. Two older capital ships were still on the Navy List in other roles: Iron Duke, a gunnery training ship from 1931 and a depot ship from 1939 [R8], and Centurion, a radio-controlled target ship from 1927 [R9]. Five King George V class battleships joined the fleet during the war, between 11 December 1940 and 29 August 1942 [R1]; Vanguard, laid down 2 October 1941, was not completed until 9 August 1946 [R6]. Five capital ships were lost to enemy action: Royal Oak at Scapa Flow on 14 October 1939 [R10], Hood in the Denmark Strait on 24 May 1941 [R20], Barham off the Egyptian coast on 25 November 1941 [R11], and Prince of Wales and Repulse off Malaya on 10 December 1941 [R12, R13]. Royal Sovereign was transferred on loan to the Soviet Navy on 31 May 1944 and served as Arkhangelsk [R16]. Centurion was scuttled on 9 June 1944 [R9]. Every other ship listed below survived to be sold for breaking up between 1946 and 1959 [R2, R3, R4, R8, R14, R15, R17, R18, R19].

## Conflicts between sources

- Nelson's disposal. navypedia gives "stricken 2.1948, sold for BU 5.1948" [R2]; naval-history.net gives the sale to BISCO on 19 March 1948 and arrival at Inverkeithing on 15 March 1949 [R17].
- Royal Sovereign's return from Soviet service. naval-history.net states she remained on loan until 9 February 1949 and then says she arrived in tow at Inverkeithing on 5 April 1948, which cannot both hold [R16]; navypedia gives the loan as August 1944 to February 1949 and breaking up in April 1949 [R4].
- King George V's end. navypedia gives "stricken 4.1957" [R1]; naval-history.net gives the Disposal List in 1957, the tow to Dalmuir on 20 January 1958, and demolition completed at Troon from May 1959 [R19].
- Renown's builder. The naval-history.net Renown page states she was laid down by Fairfield at Govan on 25 January 1915 and launched 4 March 1916, but the same sentence names the launched ship "RENOWN" as though it were the sister; the page is internally inconsistent on which yard built which ship [R14, R13].
