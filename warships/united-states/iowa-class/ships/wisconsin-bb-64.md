## What differed from the class baseline

- Her DANFS entry is the long-form one and gives her displacement as 45,000 tons standard and 57,540 tons full load, and her complement as 2,978 (maximum, 1945), figures the other three entries lack [S284].
- Flagship of Battleship Division 9 from 25 November 1944 under Rear Admiral Hanson, then Denfield from 26 April 1945 and Kingman from 11 August 1945 [S284].
- Capt. John W. Roper relieved Capt. Stone at sea off Iwo Jima on 20 February 1945 [S284].
- The DANFS entry records her fueling screening destroyers on some 250 occasions and steaming 105,831 miles by 5 September 1945 [S284]; it also records her two floatplanes launching for rescue duty on 8 and 9 June 1945 [S284].
- Ordered to bombard Camranh Bay on 12 January 1945 but found no targets [S284]; the only bombardment order in the class's record that produced no fire.
- Her bow was replaced in 1956 with the bow of the unfinished Kentucky [S286].
