# Bismarck class: sources

Tiers: 1 primary (official documents, war diaries, technical reports, builder's plans, national archives), 2 recognized reference works and technical sites that cite their documents, 3 enthusiast and museum sites. Wikipedia is not cited. Entries S1 to S37 were fetched on 2026-09-11; S15, S18, S19, S20, S26 and the new entry S38 were fetched again on 2026-09-12. KBismarck.com text is copyrighted; it is paraphrased and cited, never copied.
