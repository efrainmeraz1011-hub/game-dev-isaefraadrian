The Bundesarchiv lists Tirpitz as launched 1939, commissioned 1941 and capsized in 1944 after a Royal Air Force air attack, with the personnel files of Karl Topp and Hans Karl Gustav Meyer in its holdings [S32]. The US Naval Technical Mission report gives the loss sequence: bomb carpet at 0938, list to about 35 degrees, then 60 degrees, explosion of turret Cäsar at 0945, capsize about seven minutes after the first bombs to about 135 degrees [S18, §2].

## Conflicts between sources

- Wreck discovery date: 8 June 1989 [S27] against 9 June 1989 [S20].
- Bismarck's sinking time: 1039 [S20; S22], 1037 [S15, §VI], 1101 [S33].
- Tirpitz dead on 12 November 1944: 971 [S19] against about 1,200 [S18].
