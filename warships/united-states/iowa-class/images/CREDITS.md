# Iowa class: image credits

Every file below is a work of the United States Government. Under 17 U.S.C. § 105(a),
"Copyright protection under this title is not available for any work of the United States
Government" [S-L1], so these drawings, pamphlet figures, and photographs are in the public
domain in the United States. The plan sheets and the docking plan were drawn by US Navy
yards (Navy Yard, New York; corrected by a naval shipyard for the 1984 reactivation), the
OP 769 figures come from a US Navy Bureau of Ordnance pamphlet, the turret cutaway comes
from a US Naval Academy text printed by the US Government Printing Office, and the two
1986 turret photographs were taken by a serving US Navy photographer's mate. No file in
this folder falls outside that rule.

Three qualifications, recorded rather than smoothed over:

- The Internet Archive item pages for the plan sheets and the docking plan carry **no**
  `rights` or `licenseurl` field and display no rights statement. The only rights wording
  on the Archive side is the parent collection's description: "Collection of non copyright
  Booklets of General Plans and General Arrangement Drawings (merchant & naval ships) found
  on the internet" [S-L2]. The public-domain status therefore rests on 17 U.S.C. § 105 plus
  the US Navy title blocks visible on the sheets themselves, not on an Archive licence tag.
- `iowa-class-docking-plan-hull-form-194x.jpg` carries an EXIF string
  `copyright=Copyright (C) reserved` left by the Adobe Photoshop CS6 profile used on the
  Cruse large-format scanner. It is boilerplate from the scanning workflow. The underlying
  drawing is a Navy Yard, New York plan held by the US National Archives in Record Group 19.
- `iowa-class-16-inch-turret-cutaway-navpers-16116-b-1952.jpg` is named for NavPers 16116-B
  (1952). That attribution could **not** be confirmed. The identical illustration was found
  and fetched as Figure 7A1 of NavPers 10797-A (1957), and the row below credits the source
  that was actually verified. The file is not byte-identical to the copy at that URL, so it
  is a different scan of the same plate. Public-domain status is unaffected; the edition is
  uncertain.
- The four OP 769 figures are named `...-1967`. The source page dates the pamphlet to the
  30 April 1968 reactivation of USS New Jersey and cites it as "United States Navy, 1968".
  No page stating 1967 was found.

The source page for the OP 769 figures carries no copyright, rights, or licence statement of
any kind; its footer reads "Gene Slover's US Navy Pages — Not affiliated with the United
States Navy". Its authorship claim is the page's own citation block: "United States Navy
Bureau of Ordnance. NAVORD OP-769: Configuration of the Three Gun Turrets, USS New Jersey.
United States Navy, 1968."

All sixteen files were checked with `file` and are valid JPEG images.
