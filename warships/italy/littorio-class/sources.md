# Littorio class: sources

Tiers: 1 primary (official histories, archives, service publications, national legislation, official photographs); 2 recognized reference works and named naval historians; 3 enthusiast, association and photo-archive sites.

Wikipedia is never cited. Entries marked "via Wikipedia footnote" are books reached through a Wikipedia footnote that gave the page; the page alone is what is cited, and the fact was read in the Wikipedia sentence the footnote closes. The Wikipedia articles used as pointers, all read on 2026-09-12, were Littorio-class battleship, Italian battleship Littorio, Italian battleship Vittorio Veneto, Italian battleship Roma (1940), Italian battleship Impero and Gufo radar.

Note on regiamarina.net: the site's own class page "Battleships Littorio or Vittorio Veneto Class" reproduces the English Wikipedia article word for word, including its footnote markers, and is therefore not cited here. Only pages on that site carrying original authored content are used (S21).
