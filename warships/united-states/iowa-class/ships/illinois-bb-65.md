## What differed from the class baseline

- Designed with her sister Kentucky for all-welded construction, where the four completed ships had riveted-and-welded hulls [S290].
- Her name became available because the old battleship Illinois (Battleship No. 7) was renamed Prairie State and reclassified IX-15 on 8 January 1941 [S285].

## Ship-specific documents found

- DANFS Illinois page, one sentence on BB-65 [S285].
- NavSource photographs of the hull on 7 July 1945, of tapered side armor plates from 12.2 inches to 1 inch thick in storage, of sections of the 16-inch communication tube, and of prefabricated torpedo defense tank assemblies [S290].
