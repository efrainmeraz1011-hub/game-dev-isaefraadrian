# Iowa class: service history

Status: covered

Timeline only, 1940 to 1945, one table per ship, then a short note on the postwar fate. Sources are numbered in `sources-07.md`. The four completed ships draw on their DANFS entries [S281] [S282] [S283] [S284]; the two uncompleted hulls draw on the DANFS Illinois page [S285], the NHHC Kentucky page [S286], and NavSource [S290] [S291].

| 21 February 1944 | With TF 58 for the first strikes on Saipan, Tinian, Rota, and Guam | [S281] |

| 19 June 1944 | Battle of the Philippine Sea, in the battle line (TG 58.7 under Lee); shot down one torpedo plane and assisted with another | [S281] [S299] |
| 17 September 1944 | Supported the Peleliu landings with the 3d Fleet | [S281] |
| 10 to 20 October 1944 | Screened carriers for strikes on the Ryukyus, Formosa, Luzon, and the Leyte landing | [S281] |
| 24 to 26 October 1944 | Battle for Leyte Gulf with TF 38; on 25 October reversed course from the Northern Force toward Samar; with New Jersey formed TG 34.5 under Rear Admiral Badger, which did not reach San Bernardino Strait before Kurita entered it | [S281] [S300] |
| 15 January 1945 | Arrived San Francisco for overhaul | [S281] |
| 19 March to 15 April 1945 | Sailed for Okinawa; arrived 15 April; supported carrier operations from 24 April | [S281] |

| 17 to 18 July 1945 | Night bombardment of Hitachi, Honshu | [S281] [S284] |
| 29 August 1945 | Entered Tokyo Bay with the occupation force | [S281] |
| 2 September 1945 | Admiral Halsey's flagship for the surrender ceremony | [S281] |
| 20 September to 15 October 1945 | Departed Tokyo Bay; arrived Seattle | [S281] |

Postwar: flagship of the 5th Fleet from January 1946; decommissioned 24 March 1949; recommissioned 25 August 1951 and bombarded North Korean targets from April to October 1952; decommissioned 24 February 1958 into the Atlantic Reserve Fleet at Philadelphia [S281]. She earned nine battle stars for World War II and two for Korea [S281]. A turret explosion in Turret 2 during a 1989 firing exercise is the subject of NHHC H-Gram 029-4 [S322].

| 11 December 1944 | With TG 38.2, Halsey embarked, when Wisconsin joined | [S284] |

| Spring 1945 | Overhauled at Puget Sound Naval Shipyard; sailed 4 July for Guam | [S282] |

| 17 September 1945 | Arrived Tokyo Bay; flagship for successive commanders of naval forces in Japanese waters until relieved by Iowa on 28 January 1946 | [S282] |

Postwar: decommissioned 30 June 1948; recommissioned 21 November 1950 for Korea; decommissioned 21 August 1957; recommissioned 6 April 1968 for Vietnam; decommissioned 17 December 1969 [S289]. A fourth commission ended in February 1991, and she is a museum at Camden, New Jersey [S309].

## Kentucky (BB-66)

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 7 March 1942 | Keel laid, Norfolk Navy Yard, Portsmouth, Virginia | [S286] [S291] |

## Conflicts between sources

- Illinois cancellation: DANFS says 12 August 1945 [S285]; NavSource says 12 August in one caption and 11 August in another [S290].
- Nowaki engagement range at Truk, 17 February 1944: NHHC gives 34,000 to 39,000 yards for the fire of New Jersey and Iowa [S298]; the New Jersey museum gives a straddle at around 35,700 yards [S309].
