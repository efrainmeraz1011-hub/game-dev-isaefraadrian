US naval intelligence in February 1943 recorded the machinery as Parsons, the boilers as high-pressure water-tube three-drum, designed horsepower 130,000 and designed speed 30 knots [S17].

## Machinery arrangement

The eight boilers stood amidships in separate compartments. They fed four engine sets: two under the conning tower, driving the two outer screws, and two under the after mast, driving the two inner screws. Each engine set was a high-pressure, an intermediate-pressure and a low-pressure turbine with a reduction gear [S19].

The machinery spaces were protected by individual armoured cylinders over each boiler and each ventilator, by spaced armoured covers on the upper deck and by armoured diaphragms at the base, the whole tied to the side armour above and to the triple bottom below [S25, p. 34].

The machinery as a whole accounted for about 5.6 per cent of the total displacement [S28, pp. 169-171].

## Trials

On trials both of the first pair beat their designed power and speed at light displacement. Littorio reached 137,649 shp and 31.3 knots; Vittorio Veneto reached 133,771 shp and 31.4 knots. In service the ships averaged 28 knots. No trial figures for Roma were recorded [S29, pp. 289-290].

## Steering

Three rudders: one main rudder on the centreline in the wash of the inner screws, and two larger auxiliary rudders set well out from it in the wash of the outer screws, used for emergency steering [S19; S20].

Fuel consumption in service: Vittorio Veneto burned 48 t of oil an hour at 28 knots, against about 12 t an hour for the light cruiser Garibaldi at 25 knots [S14, note 3].

Fuel shortage, not machinery, governed how the class was used. By the end of 1942 the only battleships the Regia Marina could keep combat-ready were the three of this class, the four rebuilt older ships having been laid up for want of oil [S26, pp. 392, 404; S28, p. 178].

Endurance: 4,580 miles at 16 knots and 3,920 at 20 [S20] against 4,700 at 14 knots [S29, pp. 289-290] against the much larger US Navy estimates of 1943 [S17].
