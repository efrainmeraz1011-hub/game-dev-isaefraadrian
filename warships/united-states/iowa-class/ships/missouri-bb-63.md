## What differed from the class baseline

- Given final fitting out at San Francisco as a fleet flagship before sailing for the Pacific in December 1944 [S283]; served as temporary headquarters ship for Vice Admiral Mitscher at Ulithi in January 1945 [S283]; Halsey's 3d Fleet flagship from 18 May to 5 September 1945 [S283], embarked in her during the June 1945 typhoon [S304].
- The only Iowa-class ship struck by a kamikaze: 11 April 1945, 1443, starboard quarter, a Zeke crashed just below main deck level; its starboard wing was thrown forward and started a gasoline fire at 5-inch mount No. 3; damage superficial, no one killed or seriously injured [S283] [S303].
- Site of the surrender ceremony, 2 September 1945 [S283].
- Typhoon damage 5 to 6 June 1945 was limited to smashed topside fittings [S283].
