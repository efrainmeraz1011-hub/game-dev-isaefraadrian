# King George V class: sources

Numbered S1 upward. Tier 1 primary (official documents, transcriptions of Admiralty records, official drawings, national archive catalogue entries, official photographs with their captions). Tier 2 recognised reference works and technical pages that cite their documents. Tier 3 enthusiast or museum tables. Entries marked "via Wikipedia footnote" are books that could not be opened; the page comes from the Wikipedia footnote that pointed to them, and Wikipedia itself is not cited. Access date for every entry: 2026-09-11.
