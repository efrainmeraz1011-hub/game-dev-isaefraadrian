# King George V class: gaps

One heading per facet. Each entry holds the sub-facets the facet file records as unsourced, the search protocol run for that facet, and the sources checked.

Provenance of the "queries run" lines. For each facet the list is the set of patterns `skills/ww2-warship-research/QUERIES.md` prescribes for that facet, with the class or ship name substituted, together with any additional query actually typed on 2026-09-12, which is quoted as typed.

The registry entries checked for every facet are the United Kingdom block of `skills/ww2-warship-research/SOURCES.md` (Naval-History.net ship chronologies, Royal Navy Research Archive, The National Archives UK, NavWeaps British guns, Dreadnought Project) and the cross-nation block (NavWeaps, Wikimedia Commons, Internet Archive, navypedia, NavSource, Naval-History.net, The National Archives US, Royal Museums Greenwich, Imperial War Museums, Library of Congress Veterans History Project, Dreadnought Project, US Naval Institute, Naval Gazing), together with every entry already in `sources.md`.

## Reference works identified but not opened

A previous research session recorded nine printed reference works as source entries, with the page numbers taken from Wikipedia footnotes rather than from the books: Raven and Roberts, *British Battleships of World War Two*; Garzke and Dulin, *British, Soviet, French, and Dutch Battleships of World War II*; D. K. Brown, *Nelson to Vanguard* and *The Design and Construction of British Warships 1939-1945, Vol. 1*; Campbell, *Naval Weapons of World War Two*; Tarrant, *King George V Class Battleships*; Middlebrook and Mahoney, *Battleship*; Burt, *British Battleships 1919-1939*; and Friedman, *The British Battleship 1906-1946*.

None of them was opened in any session, so no fact in this dossier rests on one, and the entries were removed rather than left citing nothing. Where those books are the authority behind a figure, the dossier cites the page that quotes them: NavWeaps names Campbell, Raven and Roberts, Garzke and Dulin, Buxton and Hodges as its sources for the 14-inch, 5.25-inch and 2-pdr datapages and quotes Raven and Roberts directly on the 5.25-inch rate of fire [S6], [S8], [S9]. The Caird Library holdings list in the museum research guide names the same books as the printed literature on the class [S18]. Result: books not opened, 2026-09-12.
