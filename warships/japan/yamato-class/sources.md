# Yamato class: sources

Tiers: **1** primary (official documents, technical reports, war diaries, national archives). **2** recognized reference works and technical sites that cite their documents. **3** enthusiast, museum and press sites. Wikipedia is never cited; where it was used, it was used only to locate a footnote, and the entry says so.

Every entry below was fetched in this session on 2026-09-12. The US Naval Technical Mission to Japan reports are scanned page images with no text layer; they were rendered to PNG and read page by page, and page numbers in citations are the numbers printed on the report page, not PDF page numbers.

Accessed 2026-09-12. Whole record, entries from March 1937 to 31 August 1945, and the 1985 and 1999 wreck survey entries.
