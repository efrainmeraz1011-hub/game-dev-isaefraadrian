## Images not taken

- The photograph of Musashi firing her forward guns during 1942 gunnery trials, reproduced on the NavWeaps 46 cm datapage, is credited there to Robert Lundgren and to the family of Captain Tameshige Nagahashi. No open licence is stated, so it was not downloaded.
- Photographs of gun No. 23 at Kure in February 1946, on the same page, are marked as copyright Jack H. Carter, Jr. Not downloaded.
- Figures from USNTMJ reports O-45(N), S-06-2 and S-06-3 are US Government works, but they were read as page images in the working scratchpad rather than extracted and republished here. The reports themselves are linked in `sources.md`.
