# Littorio class: service history

Status: covered

Timeline only. Per-ship detail is in the ships folder.

## Construction

| Ship | Builder | Laid down | Launched | Completed | Fate | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Littorio (Italia from 1943) | Ansaldo, Genoa-Sestri Ponente | 28 October 1934 | 22 August 1937 | 6 May 1940 | stricken 1 June 1948, scrapped at La Spezia | [S25, pp. 34, 46; S20] |
| Vittorio Veneto | Cantieri Riuniti dell'Adriatico, Trieste | 28 October 1934 | 25 July 1937 | delivered 28 April 1940, completed 15 May 1940 | stricken 1 February 1948, scrapped at La Spezia | [S25, p. 46; S28, pp. 169, 172; S20] |
| Roma | Cantieri Riuniti dell'Adriatico, Trieste | 18 September 1938 | 9 June 1940 | 14 June 1942 | sunk 9 September 1943 | [S26, pp. 404, 428; S28, p. 169] |
| Impero | Ansaldo, Genoa-Sestri Ponente | 14 May 1938 | 15 November 1939 | never completed | stricken 27 March 1947, scrapped at Venice | [S28, pp. 169, 178; S20] |

The English-language reference gives Littorio's launch as 22 August 1937 and Vittorio Veneto's as 22 July 1937 in one place and 25 July 1937 in another [S28, pp. 169, 172; S25, p. 46].

## Conflicts between sources

Scrapping dates: broken up between 1952 and 1954 [S25, p. 46], against stricken 1 June 1948 for Italia and 1 February 1948 for Vittorio Veneto [S20; S7; S8].

Impero's fate: raised in October 1947 and towed to Venice to be broken up [S28, pp. 175-178], against stricken 27 March 1947 [S20], against captured incomplete on 10 September 1943 [S22].

Littorio's repairs after Taranto: completed 11 March 1941 [S28, p. 175], against under repair until April 1941 [S22].
