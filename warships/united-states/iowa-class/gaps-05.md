# Iowa class: gaps for facet 05 (compartments and deck plans)

Sub-facets with no source after the QUERIES.md patterns were run. Source numbers refer to
`sources-05.md`. All entries dated 2026-09-12.

## Armoured citadel: frame extent stated as a citadel by a source
Queries run: read every transverse bulkhead callout on the 1984 second deck, third deck and first platform plates for a material note; read the docking plan's section at frame 121 and its general notes for an armour boundary; read the 1954 damage control diagram's key, notes and twenty-four transverse sections for a citadel boundary.
Sources checked: 1984 second deck plate S187, third deck plate S189, first platform plate S190, inboard profile S183, splinter deck plate S188; 1954 damage control diagram S193; docking plan S194; Nomenclature of Naval Vessels S181.
Result: the only transverse bulkhead on any plate marked as armour is the one at frame 166, lettered "OT WT STS BH" on the first platform plate S190. The bulkhead at frame 50 is lettered "OT WT BH" and "OT WT BHD" with no material note S187 S189 S190. The word citadel appears on none of the plates. The armour scheme belongs to facet 01, which carries its own sources; this facet does not restate it. No source found for a citadel frame extent stated as such, 2026-09-12.

## Total number of compartments in the ship
Queries run: "Battleship New Jersey museum deck tour second deck third deck compartments tour"; read the Battleship New Jersey tours page in full; checked the 1984 plates and the 1954 sheet for a compartment schedule or count.
Sources checked: Battleship New Jersey tours page S196; 1984 plates S183 to S192; 1954 damage control diagram S193; Nomenclature of Naval Vessels S181.
Result: a figure of about 1,600 compartments appears in third-party travel writing quoting the museum, but no museum or official page stating it was fetched, and no compartment schedule appears on any plate. No source found, 2026-09-12.

## A WWII-dated general arrangement or booklet of general plans for any ship of the class
Queries run: checked the Internet Archive items named in the registry for BB-61 (bb61bogp1984v3, bb61gad1954, bb61classgad194x) and read their metadata for dates.
Sources checked: Internet Archive item metadata for the 1984 booklet S182; the 1954 damage control diagram S193; the docking plan S194, whose title block is marked Preliminary and undated on the sheet read.
Result: the earliest dated general arrangement drawing reached is December 1954 S193. The docking plan is the only other pre-modernization drawing and it is a hull-form and blocking drawing, not an arrangement drawing S194. No wartime general arrangement or booklet of general plans was found, so every compartment name in section 4 is labelled as 1984. 2026-09-12.

## Wartime aviation spaces and the removed gun galleries
Queries run: "Iowa class battleship 1980s modernization removed 5-inch mounts catapults aircraft crane changes from WWII arrangement"; read the 1984 main deck, 01 level and inboard profile plates for catapult, crane, aviation gasoline, 40 mm or 20 mm spaces.
Sources checked: 1984 main deck plate S186, first and superstructure deck plate S185, inboard profile S183; 1954 damage control diagram S193; Battleship New Jersey tours page S196.
Result: the 1984 plates show a helicopter deck and helicopter service area where the catapults were, and the only 40 mm space anywhere on them is the 40 mm magazine B-0104M on the 01 level S186 S185 S183. The search returned only encyclopaedia and news articles, which the standing instruction excludes. No fetchable source states which mounts, catapults or galleries were removed and when, so section 7 says only what the plates show. 2026-09-12.

## Flag quarters or admiral's cabin labelled as such on a plan
Queries run: read the 01 level and 02 level plans and the inboard profile for flag, admiral or staff quarters.
Sources checked: 1984 first and superstructure deck plate S185; 1984 bridges plate S184; 1984 inboard profile S183; 1984 main deck plate S186; Battleship New Jersey tours page S196.
Result: the main deck carries a flag operation office A-103L S186 and the 03 level is the flag and signal bridge with a flag conning station S184 S183, but no space on any plate read is lettered flag quarters or admiral's cabin. The 02 level deckhouse holds a guest stateroom B-0202L, guest bath, pantry B-0204L and a lobby, which is the only suite-shaped group found, and the plate does not say whose S185. The Battleship New Jersey museum names an Admiral's Cabin among the spaces on its tour routes but gives no deck or frame S196. No source found that ties a labelled space to flag quarters, 2026-09-12.

## The Bluejackets' Manual, 1944 edition
Queries run: fetched the HyperWar Bluejackets' Manual index and its chapter files; fetched the HyperWar USN reference document index.
Sources checked: HyperWar Bluejackets' Manual contents page S195.
Result: HyperWar holds the 1940 tenth edition, not the 1944 edition, and the chapter files under that directory returned 404 on the date of access, so no chapter text could be read S195. Nomenclature of Naval Vessels, revised February 1942, covers the same ground and is used instead S181. No 1944 edition found, 2026-09-12.

## Compartment volumes, deck heights between decks, and headroom
Queries run: read the 1984 bridges plate for level heights; read the docking plan's principal dimensions block; read the 1954 damage control diagram for a deck height schedule.
Sources checked: 1984 bridges plate S184 (gives heights above the moulded base line for the 03 to 011 levels only); docking plan S194 (frame spacing, length between perpendiculars, midship section, no deck heights); 1954 damage control diagram S193 (names the decks on the profile, prints no heights).
Result: heights above the moulded base line were found for the 03 to 011 levels and for the forward main battery director only S184. No height was found for the main, second, third, splinter or platform decks on any sheet read here, and no headroom figure for any compartment. 2026-09-12.

## Frame spacing in the fine ends
Queries run: read the docking plan's principal dimensions block and general notes; read the 1984 third deck bow inset and the first platform peak tank inset.
Sources checked: docking plan S194; 1984 third deck plate S189; 1984 first platform plate S190.
Result: the docking plan gives a single frame spacing of 4'-0" with no exception noted S194, while Nomenclature of Naval Vessels says frames are set closer together in some sections, for example at the very bow S181. Neither source states what the spacing is forward of the first watertight bulkhead on this class. 2026-09-12.
