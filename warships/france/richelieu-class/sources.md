# Richelieu class: sources

Tiers: 1 primary (official histories, archives, official photographs); 2 recognized reference works and named naval historians; 3 enthusiast, museum and photo-archive sites. Wikipedia is never cited; entries marked "via Wikipedia footnote" are books reached through a Wikipedia footnote that gave the page, and the page alone is what is cited. The Wikipedia articles used as pointers were Richelieu-class battleship, French battleship Richelieu, French battleship Jean Bart (1940) and, in French, Richelieu (cuirassé de 1939), all read on 2026-09-11.
