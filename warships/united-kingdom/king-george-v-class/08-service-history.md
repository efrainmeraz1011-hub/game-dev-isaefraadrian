## King George V

- October 1940: at Rosyth for fitting of outer propellers, rudder strengthening, and completion of armament and radar [S1].
- 2 to 4 December 1940: first-of-class and acceptance trials at Scapa; gun trials exposed defects in the loading mechanisms [S1].
- 15 to 25 January 1941: carried Lord Halifax to the United States [S18].
- 1 April 1941: flagship of Admiral Sir John Tovey, C-in-C Home Fleet, to May 1943 [S18].
- 27 May 1941: engaged Bismarck from 0848 with Rodney; fired 339 x 14-inch and 660 x 5.25-inch shells; ceased fire 1021 and withdrew low on fuel [S1].
- 1 May 1942: collided with destroyer Punjabi in fog off Iceland at 25 knots; Punjabi cut in half, 49 lost; 40 ft gash in the bow; repaired at Gladstone Dock, Liverpool, May to June 1942 [S1], [S30]. Photograph of the damaged bow [S44].
- May to August 1943: Force H for Operation Husky; 12 July 1943 bombarded Trapani, Favignana and Levanzo with Howe [S18], [S5].
- 9 September 1943: Operation Slapstick at Taranto; escorted surrendered Italian ships [S18], [S5].
- February to July 1944: refit at Liverpool for the Eastern Fleet [S18].
- November 1944: bombarded Milos, Aegean, on passage east [S18].
- 15 January 1945: first underway refuelling, 10 hours [S1]; the RMG guide gives 12 hours for the January to February 1945 passage to Sydney [S18].
- March to May 1945: Task Force 57, Operation Iceberg off the Sakishima Gunto [S1], [S18].
- 17 to 18 July 1945: night radar bombardment of the Hitachi works with US Task Unit 34.8.2; 267 x 14-inch fired [S1].
- 29 July 1945: bombarded Hamamatsu from 20,075 yards; 265 x 14-inch fired, seven hits; last British battleship main-armament firing in anger [S1], [S18].
- 2 September 1945: present at the surrender; provided chairs for the signatories [S18]. Photographed with USS Missouri on 16 August 1945 [S52].
- December 1946 refit; November 1947 training ship at Portland; June 1950 laid up at Gareloch; 17 December 1957 removed from the Navy List; 1958 broken up at Dalmuir and Troon [S18].

## Prince of Wales

- January to March 1941: fitting out at Rosyth; Type 281, Type 284 and four Type 285 radars fitted; last turret accepted from Vickers-Armstrong on 27 March 1941 [S2].
- 24 May 1941, Denmark Strait: opened fire 0553; hit by four 15-inch and three 8-inch shells; compass platform hit; turned away 0613 [S14], [S15]. Detail in 07.
- August 1941: carried the Prime Minister to Placentia Bay (Argentia) [S51].
- 25 October 1941: sailed for the Far East [S24].
- 10 December 1941: sunk off Kuantan by Japanese aircraft; four torpedo hits and one bomb hit; sank 1320 capsizing to port; 327 lost of 1,612 aboard [S2], [S24], [S25].
- 1942: Second Bucknill Committee inquiry into the loss [S28], [S29].
- 2007: Expedition Job 74 hull survey [S25]; March 2019: MOD SALMO survey found the wreck heavily damaged by illegal salvage [S26].

## Duke of York

- Completed at Rosyth, October to November 1941, with 85 per cent "hostilities only" men in her complement [S3].
- December 1941 to January 1942: carried the Prime Minister to the Arcadia conference [S3].
- 1942: Arctic convoy cover; November 1942 Operation Torch with Force H [S3].
- Rosyth refit early 1943; 24 single 20 mm Oerlikons added [S3].
- 26 December 1943, North Cape: engaged Scharnhorst from 1651 at 12,000 yards; Scharnhorst sunk [S3]. Detail in 07.
- 1944 to March 1945: refit for the Pacific; catapult removed, radar renewed [S23]; sailed for the Mediterranean with Anson 25 April 1945; held at Malta for a major electrical defect [S4].
- 1945: flagship, British Pacific Fleet; present at Tokyo Bay [S3].

## Anson

- 22 June 1942: completed; 27.62 knots on the measured mile [S4].
- 1942 to 1944: Home Fleet; distant cover for Arctic convoys PQ18, JW51B, JW52, JW54A, JW54B, JW58; cover for Tirpitz strikes April 1944 [S4].
- July 1944 to March 1945: Devonport refit; radar outfit renewed; aircraft facilities removed for accommodation and boat stowage [S4].
- 1945: British Pacific Fleet; Hong Kong reoccupation (see ships/anson.md) [S4].

## Howe

- 17 June 1942: commissioned for trials; radar fit of 14 sets completed at Rosyth by August 1942 [S5].
- 2 September 1942: full-power trial, 27.5 knots [S5].
- 1942 to 1943: Home Fleet, Arctic cover [S5].
- July 1943: Operation Husky; 12 July bombardment of Trapani; September 1943 Taranto and escort of the surrendered Italian fleet [S5].
- December 1943 to May 1944: Devonport refit; Type 274 replaced Type 284; aircraft facilities removed [S5].
- August 1944: Eastern Fleet; November 1944 British Pacific Fleet; December 1944 Sydney [S5].
- 1945: Okinawa (battle honour) [S5].

## Class fate

All four survivors stricken April 1957 [S23]; King George V towed to Gareloch June 1950 alongside Anson and Duke of York [S18].

## Conflicts between sources

- King George V completion: 11 December 1940 [S1], [S23]; the RMG guide gives commissioning 1 October 1940 and completion 1 October 1940 in its statistics block, but joining the Home Fleet 11 December 1940 [S18].
- Duke of York completion: 1 November 1941 [S3, October to November entries] and 4 November 1941 [S3, introduction], [S23].
- First underway refuelling of King George V: 10 hours [S1]; 12 hours [S18].
