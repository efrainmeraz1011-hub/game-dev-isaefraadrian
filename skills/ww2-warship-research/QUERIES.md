# Search patterns per facet

Run the registry sources for the nation first (`SOURCES.md`), then the general web. `<ship>` is the ship or class name; `<hull>` is the hull number or pennant when one exists. Fetch the top results that come from tier 1 to 3 sites; use a Wikipedia hit only to follow its footnotes.

## Resolve the subject

- `<ship> class battleship` / `<ship> <hull> navsource`
- `<ship> DANFS` (US) / `<ship> naval-history.net` (UK) / `<ship> combinedfleet tabular record` (Japan)

## 01 Hull and armor

- `<ship> booklet of general plans`
- `<ship> armor scheme belt deck barbette thickness`
- `<ship> torpedo defense system liquid layer`
- `<ship> metacentric height stability`
- `<ship> weight breakdown hull armor machinery armament`
- `<ship> displacement standard full load as designed`

## 02 Propulsion and power

- `<ship> boilers turbines shaft horsepower`
- `<ship> Babcock Wilcox` / `<ship> Kampon boilers` / `<ship> Wagner boilers` / `<ship> Admiralty three-drum boilers`
- `<ship> ship service turbogenerator kW`
- `<ship> fuel oil capacity tons endurance knots`
- `<ship> distilling plant fresh water`
- `<ship> underway replenishment fueling at sea`

## 03 Armament and ammunition

- `navweaps <gun caliber> <nation> <mark or model>`
- `<ship> turret ordnance pamphlet OP` (US) / `<ship> gun mounting handbook` (UK)
- `<ship> projectile hoist powder hoist loading cycle`
- `<ship> magazine shell room capacity rounds per gun`
- `<ship> secondary battery` / `<ship> anti-aircraft battery 1945`
- `<ship> catapult aircraft carried`

## 04 Sensors, fire control, communications

- `<ship> fire control director rangefinder`
- `<ship> radar sets 1944` / `<ship> Type 21 radar` (Japan) / `<ship> FuMO radar` (Germany) / `<ship> Type 279 radar` (UK)
- `<ship> combat information center` / `<ship> plotting room`
- `<ship> sonar` (record the documented absence for battleships when a source states it)
- `<ship> radio room transmitters antennas`

## 05 Compartments and deck plans

- `<ship> booklet of general plans` (maritime.org, HNSA, NARA)
- `<ship> compartment list` / `<ship> deck plans`
- `<ship> museum deck tour <deck name>` (museum ships publish deck-by-deck guides)
- `<ship> watertight compartments bulkheads subdivision`

## 06 Crew and daily life

- `<ship> complement officers enlisted <year>`
- `<ship> berthing bunks crew quarters`
- `<ship> galley mess deck meals per day`
- `navy ration allowance <nation> <year>` / `Cook Book of the United States Navy 1945` / `Royal Navy victualling scale 1943` / `Imperial Japanese Navy ration rice`
- `<ship> oral history crew life` (NHHC, IWM, Library of Congress Veterans History Project)
- `<ship> daily routine watch bill`

## 07 Operations and doctrine

- `<ship> general quarters battle stations organization`
- `<ship> damage control repair parties`
- `<ship> shore bombardment procedure` / `<ship> action report <battle>`
- `<nation> navy fleet tactical publication <year>` / `USF 10A` / `Fighting Instructions <year>`
- `<ship> war damage report BuShips`

## 08 Service history

- `<ship> DANFS` / `<ship> tabular record of movement` / `<ship> naval-history.net`
