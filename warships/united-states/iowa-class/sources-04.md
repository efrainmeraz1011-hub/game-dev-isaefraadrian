# Sources, facet 04 (sensors, fire control, and communications)

Numbered S141 to S158. Tiers: 1 primary, 2 reference, 3 museum or enthusiast site. Every entry was fetched on 2026-09-12.

S141. Naval Ordnance and Gunnery, NAVPERS 16116. Bureau of Naval Personnel, Training Division, May 1944. Internet Archive item navalordnanceandgunnerynavpers161161944. OCR text at https://archive.org/download/navalordnanceandgunnerynavpers161161944/Naval%20Ordnance%20and%20Gunnery%20-%20NAVPERS%2016116%20-%201944_djvu.txt; item page https://archive.org/details/navalordnanceandgunnerynavpers161161944. Tier 1. Accessed 2026-09-12. Pages used: 387 to 394 and 401 (chapter 17, dual-purpose battery fire control system, Mark 37 director, searchlight and star-shell control), 416 (chapter 17D, Mark 1 computer functions), 443 to 458 (chapter 18, main battery fire control system and turret fire control equipment), 461 to 462 and 470 to 471 (chapter 19, Mark 14 sight and Mark 51 director), 500 to 503 (chapter 22, organization, combat information center, battery control). Page images for pages 445, 446, and 449 were read directly at https://archive.org/download/navalordnanceandgunnerynavpers161161944/page/n459_w800.jpg, /page/n460_w1400.jpg, and /page/n463_w1200.jpg to resolve fractions the OCR garbles; the 26 1/2-foot Mark 38 rangefinder base length and the 6 1/2-foot director height come from the page image of page 446.
S142. Fire Control Equipment, Fire Control Radar, Mark 8, Operation. Ordnance Pamphlet No. 658, Bureau of Ordnance, US Navy, January 1943, classified CONFIDENTIAL when issued. Scanned at Gene Slover's US Navy Pages. Page https://www.eugeneleeslover.com/ENGINEERING/OP658.php; PDF https://www.eugeneleeslover.com/ENGINEERING/OP658.pdf. Tier 1. Accessed 2026-09-12. Pages used: preface, 1 to 7 (introduction, characteristic properties, principal units), plate 1 (cutaway of the Mark 38 director).
S143. Radar Operator's Manual, Radar Bulletin No. 3 (RADTHREE), with Change 1 of April 1945. Commander in Chief, United States Fleet, Headquarters, Navy Department, promulgated 5 August 1944; classified CONFIDENTIAL when issued. Transcribed by the Maritime Park Association. https://www.maritime.org/doc/radar/index.htm. Tier 1. Accessed 2026-09-12. Parts used: part 2 (general IFF principles, Mark III IFF, BL/BN interrogator-responsor, BK and ABK transpondors, antennas), part 4 (SG radar controls, operational technique, and performance; SC and SK radar composition and performance; Mark 3 and Mark 4 radar controls, performance, accuracy table from BuOrd Pamphlet 657), part 5 (relative motion and combat information center: object, functions, information lists, typical layouts). The online text is OCR and two rows of the SK maximum-range table are transcribed with figures that do not fit their column; those rows are not used.
S144. Introduction to Radio Equipment, NAVPERS 10172. Standards and Curriculum Division, Training, Bureau of Naval Personnel, edition of 1946. Transcribed by the Maritime Park Association. https://www.maritime.org/doc/radio/index.htm. Tier 1. Accessed 2026-09-12. Chapters used: 22 (Navy transmitters: TBK and TBM, TBL, TDE, TBS), 23 (Navy receivers: RAK and RAL, RBA).
S145. Gun Fire Control System Mark 37, Operating Instructions. Ordnance Pamphlet 1719, Bureau of Ordnance, Navy Department, 21 April 1949, classified RESTRICTED when issued. HyperWar (ibiblio), transcribed by Patrick Clancey. https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/USN/ref/Ordnance/FCS-Mark37/index.html. Tier 1. Accessed 2026-09-12. Index page only: the promulgating letter with its list of component pamphlets (OP 1060 gun director Mark 37, OP 1063 stable element Mark 6, OP 1064 computer Mark 1, OP 1076 and OP 1772 radar equipment Mark 12, OP 1153 and OP 1775 radar equipment Mark 22, OP 1651 and OP 1788 radar equipment Mark 25) and the table of contents, which shows chapter 3 handling visible air targets with radar Mark 12 and obscured air targets with radar Mark 12 and Mark 22.
S146. Battleship Photo Index BB-61 IOWA. NavSource Naval History. https://www.navsource.net/archives/01/61a.htm. Tier 3. Accessed 2026-09-12. Dated photograph captions, many contributed by Pieter Bakels, with text identifications credited to Arthur Cappabianca, Rob McCune, and Robert M. Cieri, and with several captions drawn from Norman Friedman, U.S. Battleships: An Illustrated Design History. Captions used: pre-commissioning early 1943 (Mark 8 on Spot 2, Mark 4 and SG not yet fitted, Mark 51 directors with Mark 14 Mod 2 sights, 36-inch searchlights each side of the stack, antenna trunks on the flag bridge level); 22 February 1943 commissioning (Mark 38 with Mark 8 aft); 1943 shakedown (Mark 14 sight on a 20 mm gun); 28 March 1943 inclining (conning tower levels, fire control station, chart room, air defence station, 24-inch searchlight, target designators); November 1943 (four 5-inch directors, Mark 37 radar equipment); 8 July 1945 (SK, SR, SG, SU, DBA, TBS, AS-56, BK-7, TDY, Mark 8 and Mark 8 Mod 3, Mark 4/22); 20 August 1945 (Mark 27, Mark 8 Mod 1, BL-5, Nancy beacons, Mark 57 directors, SPT-4 and AS-37 on Missouri); mid-1946 and late 1948 (SK-2, SC-2); early 1952 (SPS-6, SG-6, SP, DBM); the Mark 40 director block; the Mark 27 radar equipment photographs; the Mark 51 Mod and Mod 3 photographs; the Derby and Sword antenna caption.
S147. Battleship Photo Index BB-62 NEW JERSEY. NavSource Naval History. https://www.navsource.net/archives/01/62a.htm. Tier 3. Accessed 2026-09-12. Captions used: SG on the forward face of the forward fire control tower; 29 October 1943 Mark 49 director photographs from the records of Puget Sound Naval Shipyard, Seattle NARA, via researcheratlarge.com and Pieter Bakels, with the Mark 11 and Mark 19 radar note; the 1943 to 1944 foremast caption (SK only, second SG topmast added later); 24 June 1945 inclining at Puget Sound (conning tower periscopes, Mark 27, Mark 57 directors, Mark 51 splinter shield on No. 3 turret); 1945 post-overhaul (SK-2 and SP, new mainmast); early 1950s (Mark 12/22 and Mark 25, SPS-6, Mark 13, whip and TBS antennas, ski-pole IFF, Derby); 9 December 1983 forward secondary battery plot room.
S148. Battleship Photo Index BB-63 MISSOURI. NavSource Naval History. https://www.navsource.net/archives/01/63a.htm. Tier 3. Accessed 2026-09-12. Captions used: 23 July 1944 at New York Navy Yard (enclosed square-faced bridge, Mark 27 replacing the Mark 3 atop the conning tower, 36-inch searchlight abaft it); about August 1944 shakedown (36-inch searchlight crew); 17 September 1950 main battery plotting room.
S149. Battleship Photo Index BB-64 WISCONSIN. NavSource Naval History. https://www.navsource.net/archives/01/64a.htm. Tier 3. Accessed 2026-09-12. Captions used: 1951 (SK-2 carried until decommissioning in 1948, SPS-6 after recommissioning); 21 March 1952 (Mark 38 main battery director and various radar antennas).
S150. IOWA battleships (1943 - 1944). Navypedia. http://www.navypedia.org/ships/usa/us_bb_iowa.htm. Tier 3. Accessed 2026-09-12. Electronic equipment line for each of BB-61 to BB-64 as commissioned, and the dated modernization list from July 1943 through the 1980s, from which the 1945 and January 1946 radar fits are taken.
S151. USA 16"/50 (40.6 cm) Mark 7. Tony DiGiulian. NavWeaps. http://www.navweaps.com/Weapons/WNUS_16-50_mk7.php. Tier 2. Accessed 2026-09-12. Turret rangefinder paragraph: 25 power, 46-foot base, Mark 52 stereoscopic in turrets II and III, Mark 53 coincidence in turret I, Mark 52 weight and focus limits.
S152. Bofors 40 mm L/60 Model 1936 (USA 40 mm/56.3 Mark 1, Mark 2 and M1). Tony DiGiulian. NavWeaps. http://www.navweaps.com/Weapons/WNUS_4cm-56_mk12.php. Tier 2. Accessed 2026-09-12. Sections used: Mark 51 director system and the October 1944 to February 1945 kill credit; maximum effective range under Mark 51 against Mark 56 and Mark 63 control; mount crew and Mark 51 director crew; the asterisked mount modifications carrying a radar antenna for the Mark 63 director; the Mark 49 photograph caption from Puget Sound Naval Shipyard records naming USS New Jersey.
S153. History and Technology, The Mark 51 FCS. Gene Slover. NavWeaps. http://www.navweaps.com/index_tech/tech-049.php. Tier 2. Accessed 2026-09-12. Whole page: Mark 51 construction, the Mark 14 gyro sight, caging trigger, fixed 5,000-yard range input, tracer correction, and the statement that adding radar and a range input produced the Mark 57.

S155. Some Common Questions Asked by Visitors to the BB-62, with the USS New Jersey ship's antennas article. Battleship New Jersey Amateur Radio Station radio room restoration group, undated. https://www.navy-radio.com/ships/bb62/bb62-faq-sheet.pdf. Tier 3. Accessed 2026-09-12. Answer to question 2 (the pre-1980s radio room on the third deck just aft of the No. 2 barbette, near sick bay; the present main deck radio room, FACCON 1, and FACCON 2 and what those spaces were before); the antenna article's colour coding and antenna list, used only to date the present arrangement.
S156. BNJARS, Reference Information for Members. Battleship New Jersey Amateur Radio Station. https://www.nj2bb.org/reference/index.html. Tier 3. Accessed 2026-09-12. The list of member reference documents: arrangement drawings of the Combat Engagement Center, CIC, Radio Central and the transmitter room; the compartment numbering system; the list of communications spaces; the antenna list. Used only to record what the preserved ship's spaces are called.
