# Iowa class: sources for facet 05 (compartments and deck plans)

Entries S181 to S196. Tier 1 is an official drawing, manual, or history; tier 2 a named naval
historian or a technical page that cites its documents; tier 3 a museum or enthusiast site.
Sheet images of the 1984 Booklet of General Plans were downloaded at full scan resolution from the
Internet Archive item and read in crops; each plate is listed separately because each is a distinct
drawing with its own title block.

S181. Nomenclature of Naval Vessels, revised February 1942 (second edition, first printing June 1942). Navy Department, Division of Personnel Supervision and Management, with the Bureau of Ships. Transcribed by Patrick Clancey, HyperWar Foundation, ibiblio.org. https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/USN/ref/NNV/index.html. Tier 1. Accessed 2026-09-12. Introduction (pp. VII to IX); "Nomenclature of Decks and Numbering of Watertight Compartments" (pp. 1 to 4); "Nomenclature of Machinery Spaces" (p. 4); "Numbering Compartments" (pp. 5 to 6); abbreviations table (p. 6).
