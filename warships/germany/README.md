# Kriegsmarine battleships and battlecruisers, 1939 to 1945

In September 1939 the Kriegsmarine had two capital ships in commission, Scharnhorst completed 7 January 1939 and Gneisenau completed 21 May 1938 [R1, R7], together with the three Deutschland class armoured ships completed between 1 April 1933 and 6 January 1936 [R3, R8] and two surviving Deutschland class pre-dreadnought battleships of 1908, Schlesien and Schleswig-Holstein, by then training ships [R4]. Schleswig-Holstein fired the first shots of the war at the Polish fortification on the Westerplatte on 1 September 1939 [R4]. Two Bismarck class battleships were completed during the war, Bismarck on 24 August 1940 and Tirpitz on 25 February 1941 [R2, R7]. No German capital ship survived the war in commission. Admiral Graf Spee was scuttled off Montevideo on 17 December 1939 [R3, R8]; Bismarck was sunk on 27 May 1941 [R2, R7]; Gneisenau was decommissioned on 1 July 1942 and scuttled at Gotenhafen on 27 March 1945 [R7]; Scharnhorst was sunk after the battle off the North Cape on 26 December 1943 [R1, R7]; Tirpitz was capsized by Lancaster bombers at Tromsø on 12 November 1944 [R2, R7]; Schleswig-Holstein was sunk at Gotenhafen on 18 December 1944 [R4]; Admiral Scheer was sunk at Kiel on 9 April 1945 [R3, R8]; and Lützow, Schlesien and their remaining consorts were destroyed in the Baltic in the first days of May 1945 [R3, R4, R8].

## How sources classify the Deutschland class of 1933 to 1936

The three ships Deutschland (renamed Lützow in January 1940), Admiral Scheer and Admiral Graf Spee are filed differently by the two sources fetched. KBismarck.com groups them as Panzerschiffe, translating the term as armoured ships, on a page separate from both its battleship and its battlecruiser pages [R8]. navypedia files the same three ships as the DEUTSCHLAND heavy cruisers of 1933 to 1936, in its cruiser section and not in its capital ship section [R3]. They are listed in this roster because a battleship roster that omitted them would be read as an omission, and the row states both classifications. The unrelated pre-dreadnought Deutschland class of 1906 to 1908 is filed by navypedia as battleships [R4].

## Conflicts between sources

- Gneisenau's end. navypedia records only that she was damaged on 12 February 1942 and that her repair was cancelled in July 1942 [R1]; KBismarck.com gives decommissioning on 1 July 1942 and scuttling at Gotenhafen on 27 March 1945 [R7].
- Scharnhorst class type. navypedia titles the class "SCHARNHORST battlecruisers (1938 - 1939)" [R1]; KBismarck.com lists the same two ships under Kriegsmarine Battleships (Schlachtschiffe) [R7].
- Deutschland class of 1933 type. KBismarck.com files the three ships as Panzerschiffe [R8]; navypedia files them as heavy cruisers [R3].
- Scharnhorst's laying down. navypedia gives 16 May 1935 [R1]; KBismarck.com gives 15 June 1935 [R7]. Gneisenau: navypedia gives 3 May 1935 [R1], KBismarck.com 6 May 1935 [R7].
- H class ship list. navypedia lists H, J, K, M and N [R5]; KBismarck.com lists H, J, K, L, M and N [R7].
