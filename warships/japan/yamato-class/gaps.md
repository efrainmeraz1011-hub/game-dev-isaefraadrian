# Yamato class: gaps

One entry per sub-facet for which no source was found. The queries and the registry sources checked are recorded so the next session does not repeat them. Date of the search is 2026-09-12 throughout.

The governing fact behind most of these: the Japanese destroyed the design drawings and records for this class. Report O-45(N) states that all Japanese documents and drawings for the 46 cm mount were destroyed by bombing or deliberately burned, and that the report was reconstructed from sketches drawn from memory [S11, p. 7]. Report S-06-3 states that the majority of the Navy Ministry's wartime records were burned either when the ministry was destroyed by fire in the spring of 1945 or by order between 15 and 17 August 1945 [S13, p. 5]. No booklet of general plans for any ship of the class is known to survive.

## 01 Weight breakdown by group

Queries run: `Yamato weight breakdown hull armor machinery armament`; the S-01-3 hull design report and the S-06-2 damage report were read for a weight statement; navypedia's project history and protection sections were read in full.
Sources checked: USNTMJ S-06-2, USNTMJ S-01-3, USNTMJ O-45(N), navypedia, combinedfleet class page, Yamato Museum.
Result: no group weight breakdown found, 2026-09-12. The only figures recovered are navypedia's total armour mass of 22,534 tons and the turret weight breakdown in O-45(N), both of which are in the dossier.

## 02 Boiler and turbine makers

Queries run: read USNTMJ S-01-2 in full and S-01-12 via its contents list, looking for a manufacturer against the Yamato-class plant.
Sources checked: USNTMJ S-01-2, USNTMJ S-01-12, navypedia.
Result: no maker named, 2026-09-12. S-01-12 calls the boiler only "the standard, or KAMPON, boiler" and attributes no manufacturer [S17, p. 7]. navypedia gives "Kampon geared steam turbines" and "Kampon boilers", which is the type name, not a firm [S28].

## 02 Propeller diameter, pitch and number of blades

Queries run: read USNTMJ S-01-2 and S-01-12 for propeller dimensions, for any ship.
Sources checked: USNTMJ S-01-2, USNTMJ S-01-12, navypedia, combinedfleet, Yamato Museum.
Result: no propeller dimension is printed in either Technical Mission report, for any ship, 2026-09-12. The Yamato Museum gives a propeller diameter of 5 m [S9]; that figure appears in the dossier attributed to the museum, and no tier 1 or tier 2 source fetched confirms it. Pitch and blade count were not found anywhere.

## 02 Total boiler heating surface

Queries run: read the boiler construction section of USNTMJ S-01-12 in full.
Sources checked: USNTMJ S-01-12.
Result: the only heating surface figure printed is the air heater's, about 4,700 square feet [S17, p. 12]. No total boiler heating surface found, 2026-09-12.

## 02 Electrical plant: generator count, rating, voltage, current type

Queries run: read USNTMJ S-01-2 section on generators and S-01-12 Part VII on auxiliary machinery.
Sources checked: USNTMJ S-01-2, USNTMJ S-01-12, navypedia.
Result: neither report gives a number, rating, voltage or current type for the Yamato class, 2026-09-12. They give only the generators' location relative to the machinery spaces and the turbo-versus-diesel duty split. The relevant Technical Mission report is S-01-5, "Shipboard Electrical Equipment", 392 pages, which was not fetched.

## 02 Distilling plant capacity

## 02 Steering gear and air compressors

Queries run: read USNTMJ S-01-2 and S-01-12 in the sections covering auxiliary machinery.
Sources checked: USNTMJ S-01-2, USNTMJ S-01-12.
Result: neither report discusses steering gear or air compressors, 2026-09-12. navypedia gives armour thicknesses for the main and reserve steering gear spaces, which are in facet 01; the machinery itself is undocumented in the sources fetched. combinedfleet records that Yamato's main and auxiliary rudder controls were improved in the July 1943 docking without describing them [S1].

## 02 Underway replenishment arrangements

Queries run: searched the Yamato and Musashi tabular records for fuelling-at-sea entries.

Result: the records show transfers of fuel between ships and from tankers, which are in facet 07, but no source describes a rig or method, 2026-09-12.

## 03 Magazine and shell room stowage counts for the secondary and anti-aircraft batteries

Queries run: read USNTMJ O-45(N) (main battery only), O-47(N)-1, O-47(N)-2 and the NavWeaps datapages for a per-ship magazine capacity.
Sources checked: USNTMJ O-45(N), USNTMJ O-47(N)-1, USNTMJ O-47(N)-2, NavWeaps 15.5 cm, 12.7 cm and 25 mm datapages.
Result: only per-gun stowage figures were found (150 rounds for the 15.5 cm, 300 for Yamato's 12.7 cm, 2,000 for the 25 mm), never a per-ship magazine capacity, 2026-09-12.

## 03 Dye bag colours in the primary record

Queries run: read USNTMJ O-19's armour-piercing projectile section including the painting and marking passage.
Sources checked: USNTMJ O-19, USNTMJ O-45(N), NavWeaps.
Result: O-19 gives only the painting convention, that a dye-loaded round carries a band of the dye's colour just aft of the green tip, and names no colours [S21, p. 32]. The Yamato yellow and Musashi green assignment in the dossier rests on NavWeaps alone [S5], 2026-09-12.

## 03 Burster explosive sensitivity in the primary record

Queries run: searched USNTMJ O-19 Parts I to VIII for "Type 91 explosive", shimose, picric acid sensitivity and any sensitivity figure.
Sources checked: USNTMJ O-19.
Result: O-19 names trinitroanisol, picric acid and trinitrotoluene as burster explosives but prints no sensitivity figure, does not use the term "Type 91 explosive" and does not name shimose, 2026-09-12. The shock sensitivity of TNA and the shock-absorbing liner in the dossier rest on NavWeaps [S5].

## 03 Date of the O-47(N)-2 shipboard 25 mm installation table

## 05 Location of plotting rooms, sick bay, galleys, berthing and stores by deck

Queries run: read USNTMJ O-45(N) Figures 12 and 15 and S-06-2 in full for named spaces; read the Japanese naval periodical account of the galleys.
Sources checked: USNTMJ O-45(N), USNTMJ S-06-2, combinedfleet, Ships of the World.
Result: partial only, 2026-09-12. The ratings' galley on the upper deck amidships to starboard, the officers' galley to port, the refrigerator on the No. 2 hold deck and the provision storerooms across four decks are documented and are in facet 06 [S39]. The plotting rooms, sick bay and berthing are named as compartments in damage narratives but never located by deck.

## 06 An Imperial Japanese Navy ration scale itemised by component in grams or ounces

Queries run: `"Handbook on Japanese Military Forces" TM-E 30-480 rations ration scale grams hyperwar`; `"Imperial Japanese Navy" sailors daily ration grams rice barley shipboard galley messing research paper`; `日本海軍 艦内 食事 一日 定量 グラム 主食 副食 給与令 戦艦 烹炊所`; NDL Search for 海軍割烹術参考書.
Sources checked: HyperWar (ibiblio) TM-E 30-480 index and Chapter VIII in full, pwencycl.kgbudge.com, ships-net.co.jp, mamor-web.jp, trafficnews.jp, ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp, the fourteen USNTMJ reports held locally.
Result: no naval scale found, 2026-09-12. The only itemised table with weights reached is the US War Department's Figure 154 in TM-E 30-480, which is an Army table and is labelled as such in facet 06. The Japanese naval source states that standard quantities were fixed for the meat, fish and vegetables of the side dishes but does not print them [S39, p. 106]. TM-E 30-480 has no navy chapter; its only naval item is a section on naval land forces. 海軍割烹術参考書 (1908) exists only as bibliographic records and modern reprints, with no digitised full text reached, and it predates the war by a generation.

## Position and attitude of the Musashi wreck

Queries run: `Paul Allen expedition Musashi wreck announcement official site 2015 Sibuyan Sea`; `usni news Musashi wreck Paul Allen 2015 battleship found video`; the Yamato Museum site search at `https://yamato-museum.com/?s=` for 武蔵, 海底調査, 大和 海底 and 沈没船.
Sources checked: National Geographic, CNN, NBC News and Associated Press, the Paul G. Allen press release of 11 March 2015, the Yamato Museum Director's Notes.
Result: no latitude and longitude and no statement of how the wreck lies were found, 2026-09-12. The only disposition statement is that the wreck landed in pieces on the sea floor [S33]. No naval-history institution's write-up of the expedition was found. The two depth figures in the coverage disagree and both are recorded in `ships/musashi.md`.
