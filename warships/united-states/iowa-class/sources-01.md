# Iowa class: sources for facet 01 (hull and armor)

Entries S1 to S35. Tier 1 is an official drawing, manual, or history; tier 2 a named naval historian or a technical page that cites its documents; tier 3 a museum or enthusiast site. Entries marked "via Wikipedia footnote" are books that could not be opened; the page numbers come from the footnotes of the Wikipedia article "Iowa-class battleship" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa-class_battleship, read 2026-09-11 for its footnotes only), which is not itself cited anywhere in the dossier. That article's footnote to "Rogers n.d." led to S25, which was fetched and is cited directly.
