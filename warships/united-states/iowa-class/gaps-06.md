## Battle rations or feeding at general quarters
Queries run: `Iowa class battleship battle rations general quarters sandwiches coffee 1944`; `US Navy battleship 1944 feeding crew at general quarters battle ration` (budget exhausted, not run)
Sources checked: the list above. The Bluejackets' Manual chapter 29 lists battle stations and the battle bill but says nothing about feeding; NAVPERS 10505 and the 1944 cook book contain no occurrence of "general quarters" or "battle ration"; the 1944 booklet of general plans names no battle ration stowage. A Naval Gazing comment thread mentions sandwiches as an improvised fallback after damage, which is a reader exchange and not a source for doctrine.
Result: no source found, 2026-09-12.

## Fresh water rationing, water hours, or the daily allowance per man
Queries run: `Iowa class battleship fresh water allowance per man water hours rationing`; `USS Iowa evaporators fresh water crew gallons per man per day`
Sources checked: the list above. The crew handbook gives total distilling output of 100,000 gallons a day for boilers and crew and the HAER record gives 777 tons of potable water tankage, but neither divides that by the crew or describes rationing; the Bluejackets' Manual chapters read here do not cover water hours.
Result: no source found, 2026-09-12.

## A dedicated chapel compartment
Queries run: `USS Iowa battleship chapel divine services compartment 1944`; `Iowa class battleship chaplain office chapel general plans` (budget exhausted, not run)
Sources checked: the list above. The 1944 booklet of general plans names a chaplain's office on the main deck and a chaplain's stateroom on the first superstructure deck but no chapel on plates 6, 7, 8 or 10; the New Jersey cruise book shows the chaplains and a photograph captioned "A Jerseyman Prays" but names no space; the Bluejackets' Manual refers to Divine Services without naming a compartment.
Result: no source found, 2026-09-12.

## Complement split into officers and enlisted for New Jersey, Missouri and Wisconsin
Queries run: `USS Missouri 1945 complement officers enlisted Marines number`; `USS Wisconsin BB-64 complement 1945 officers enlisted`
Sources checked: the list above. Only the Iowa crew handbook splits a figure into officers, enlisted and Marines, and it does so for Iowa alone; the HAER record gives Missouri a single total for 1945 and for 1988; DANFS and NavSource give single totals.
Result: no source found, 2026-09-12.

## Ship's newspaper title, print run and the print shop's output
Queries run: `Iowa class battleship ship's newspaper print shop 1944 name`
Sources checked: the list above. The 1944 booklet of general plans names a print shop on the third deck aft; the Bluejackets' Manual says the ship's newspaper, if there is one, is produced under the chaplain's direction; neither names a paper nor gives a print run, and the New Jersey cruise book does not cover the print shop separately.
Result: no source found, 2026-09-12.
