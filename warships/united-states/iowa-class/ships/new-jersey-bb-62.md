## What differed from the class baseline

- Fleet flagship more than any sister. Spruance broke his flag in her at Majuro on 4 February 1944, shifting from Indianapolis, and used her until 10 April 1944 [S298] [S309]; Halsey used her as 3d Fleet flagship from 24 August 1944 to 27 January 1945 [S282] [S309]; she was 5th Fleet flagship again under Spruance from 14 August 1945 and flagship for the commanders in Japanese waters until 28 January 1946 [S282].
- Her flag quarters were enlarged and updated at Pearl Harbor in August 1944 because Spruance's arrangement was too small for Halsey's staff [S309].
- The museum history gives her original fuel oil capacity as over 7,250 tons (over 2,175,000 gallons) and an endurance of over 15,000 nautical miles at 15 knots, enough to refuel smaller ships (source undated) [S309]; facet 02 carries the primary figures.
- Her planned 1.1-inch quadruple mounts were dropped during construction in favor of 40 mm quadruple mounts, sixteen and then twenty, with forty-nine single 20 mm guns, some replaced by eight twin 20 mm mounts by 1945 (museum history, tier 3) [S309]; facet 03 carries the primary figures.
- Overhauled at Puget Sound Naval Shipyard in the final months of the war, sailing 4 July 1945 [S282].
