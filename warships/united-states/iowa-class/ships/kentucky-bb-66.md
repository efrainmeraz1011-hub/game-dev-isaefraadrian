## What differed from the class baseline

- Designed for all-welded construction with Illinois [S290]; her lower and upper armor belts were joined with pin brackets, visible in a 4 July 1945 photograph, where the first four ships used key ways [S291].
- Her forward armor bulkhead, photographed 4 February 1946, is five pieces of armor on a sixth horizontal piece, tapering from 14.5 inches at the top [S291].
- Guided-missile conversion schemes were considered and not pursued [S286].
- Her bow was removed in 1956 to repair Wisconsin, and her engines went into Sacramento (AOE-1) and Camden (AOE-2) [S286].
